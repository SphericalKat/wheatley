## What Wheatley does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Always on, locally owned" icon="laptop">
    Run Wheatley on a laptop, home server, or VPS. Your config, conversations,
    and memory stay in plain files and SQLite under `~/.wheatley`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bring your own models" icon="setting">
    Use Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, xAI, DeepSeek, or
    an OpenAI-compatible proxy. [Connect your AI
    model](/wheatley/guides/models-and-agents/) or run multiple isolated
    assistants side by side.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chat from anywhere" icon="comment">
    Talk to your assistant in the terminal, in the built-in web dashboard, or
    through [Telegram, Slack, and Discord](/wheatley/guides/frontends/). Approve
    or deny sensitive actions directly in chat.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Extend when you need more" icon="puzzle">
    Attach [MCP servers, Markdown skills, or TypeScript
    plugins](/wheatley/guides/extensions/), and automate work with [schedules
    and webhooks](/wheatley/guides/triggers/).
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
