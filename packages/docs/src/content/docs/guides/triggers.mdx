Your assistant does not have to wait for you to message it. With **triggers**, it can wake up on its own and send you a message in your chosen chat when something needs attention.

Wheatley supports three kinds of triggers:

- **Recurring schedule (`cron`)**: Runs on a five-field cron schedule in your time zone (for example, every weekday at 9:00 AM).
- **One-time reminder (`once`)**: Runs once at a specific date and time, or after a delay.
- **Webhook (`webhook`)**: Starts when an external service (such as CI, GitHub, or a monitoring tool) sends an HTTP `POST` request to Wheatley.

## Create a reminder or recurring schedule

The easiest way is to just ask your assistant in chat:

> "Remind me every weekday at 09:00 to review my open pull requests."

Your assistant can create, list, and cancel its own scheduled triggers. You can also create and inspect them visually in the web dashboard under **Triggers and Extensions** -> **New Trigger**.

<Aside type="note">
  For security, an assistant cannot create or delete `webhook` triggers on its own — only you can manage those in the dashboard.
</Aside>

## Trigger your assistant with a webhook

Wheatley's built-in webhook listener is turned off by default. When enabled, it listens on `127.0.0.1:8788` (put a reverse proxy like Caddy or Nginx in front if you want to expose it over HTTPS).

<Steps>

1. **Turn on the webhook listener in `config.yaml`** and restart the daemon:

   ```yaml
   webhooks:
     enabled: true
     port: 8788
   ```

2. **Create a secret bearer token in `secrets.yaml`**

   ```yaml
   MY_HOOK_TOKEN: <long-random-token>
   ```

3. **Create the webhook trigger in the dashboard**

   Open **Triggers and Extensions**, click **New Trigger**, choose the **Webhook** schedule, and set **Token secret** to `MY_HOOK_TOKEN`.

4. **Copy the trigger ID**

   Ask your assistant to list its triggers, or copy the `id` from the trigger you just created.

5. **Send a test `POST` request**

   ```bash
   curl -X POST \
     -H "Authorization: Bearer <long-random-token>" \
     --data 'Build 42 failed on main' \
     http://127.0.0.1:8788/webhooks/<agent>/<triggerId>
   ```

</Steps>

Wheatley replies immediately with `202 Accepted` and appends your request body (up to 256 KiB) to the trigger prompt before starting the run.
